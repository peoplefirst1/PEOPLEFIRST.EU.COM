01 The house

We make fewer clothes, and make every one of them count.

  • No mass production.
  • Limited runs, made in-house.
  • Local artisans, paid the real cost of making.
  • Italian deadstock: the cloth decides how many exist.
  • Every piece numbered. Every owner knows how few there are.

Fashion that serves both the wearer and the world. Cut to flatter every body type, easy to care for and made to outlast the season.

02 The founder

Fifteen years in fashion taught him what to leave out.

“After working with clothes for more than fifteen years, I can confidently say that there is no need to implement rocket science into your designs. Everything needs to be understandable, and clothes are no exception.

I wanted to create a brand where you can buy five pieces and be fully prepared for any occasion.”

Ivan Suprun · Founder & Creative Director

04 A size of their own

One piece. Every size in the family.

Any piece from the collection can be produced in a children’s size, cut to individual measurements.

Not a smaller version scaled to a standard size chart. The same process we use for adults: we take the child’s measurements and build the piece to fit exactly, with the same cut, the same fabric, the same attention to detail.

  • The simplest way to build
    a true family look:
    parents and children in the same piece, each in a size of their own.
05 How it is made

Made in numbers you can count.

Cloth
Italian deadstock.Fine fabric left over from larger productions. Less waste, and a run that ends when the cloth does.
Hands
Small local ateliers.Shirts shaped by true professionals with time-honoured techniques, paid the real cost of the work.
Number
A postcard with every piece.Your number, and how many were ever made in that exact pattern. A garment to pass on.
06 The people behind it

A brother, a sister and one simple idea.

“People are at the centre of everything we do. We support local artisans by paying the real cost of making, not a cost cut down by mass production.”

Sonya Suprun · Co-founder & CEO
